# Superseded (original submission)

The files in this folder belong to the original submission of EAI-D-26-00883.
They were produced with wind speeds that the source records in miles per hour
but that were read as metres per second, and with a demand profile without a
daily cycle. They are kept for transparency only.

The revised analysis (R1), including the rolling backtest on 310 observed days,
is in `revision/`; see `revision/README.md`.
